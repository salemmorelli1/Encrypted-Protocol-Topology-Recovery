Overall experiment conclusion

Across the completed 1,800-run matched-generator factorial, the additional temporal and variational complexity of the Hawkes-flow DSBM did not improve link-discrimination performance. The static GAE–Louvain comparator achieved higher link AUC in all nine perturbation-by-sparsity conditions, while the Hawkes-flow DSBM required 11.4148 to 15.2479 additional milliseconds per event. Binary log-score differences were smaller and condition-dependent, with seven of nine contrasts significant after Holm adjustment.

Within this controlled synthetic design, the simpler static model provided the stronger accuracy–cost tradeoff. This conclusion is limited to the frozen exponential-Hawkes experiment. The five-generator robustness extension remains pending, and the study supports no conclusion about real communications, identity, intent, attribution, content recovery, or operational SIGINT performance.
